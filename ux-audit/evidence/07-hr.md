Auditor: read-only. Date 6 Oct 2026, ~13:50-14:25 WIB. Rig user: Rina Kusuma (HR manager), 5 employees, 2 units (Head Office, Cikupa).

## How this was done, and what I could not see

- Live DOM + computed-style sweep of 44 People routes (the 36 requested plus group, levels, mobile, absence, rates, benefit settings, claim-allowances, annual-tax, app) and 9 /me routes at 1440x900 and 414x896 (text, headings, button sizes, fonts, backgrounds, horizontal-scroll containers). One employee sheet (Nina Oktaviani, all 10 tabs) opened at desk, tab strip checked at phone.
- Code read of `frontend-hub/src/screens/people/*`, `screens/me/*`, `components/ui/{field,fab,page-header,pill,rows,states}.tsx`, `lib/queries/people.ts`, `lib/format.ts` to confirm what the DOM showed and to cover what the rig has no data for.
- **Screenshots were not possible** (the Browser pane stayed hidden; only one screenshot succeeded at the start). Every "visual" statement is from geometry/computed style/code, not from looking at pixels.
- **The rig is almost empty**: no leave, claims, overtime orders, loans, corrections, swaps, payroll runs. So approval sheets and create-forms were NOT opened live; their wording, order and footer placement come from code (`requests.tsx`, `time-off.tsx`, `changes.tsx`, `claims.tsx`, `overtime.tsx`, `attendance-correction-sheet.tsx`, `settings-kit.tsx`). Phone rendering of those sheets is code-only.
- **The session died at ~14:15** (a 401 and the sign-in page appeared on every route). I did not sign in again (not mine to do). So all phone sweeps are complete, but follow-up live checks (phone forms, sheet footers) were not possible.
- **Source is being edited under me.** Between my desk sweep (~13:55) and 14:16 someone touched `settings-company`, `settings-departments`, `settings-levels`, `settings-shifts`, `settings-leave-types`, `settings-holidays`, `roster`, `attendance-days`, `one-offs`, `pay`, `reprimands`, `job-roles`, and was clearly converting Indonesian copy to English (e.g. roster weekday headers I saw live as "Sen Sel Rab" are `en-GB` in code now; company label "Kode badan usaha BPJS Kesehatan" is English in code). Where a string below is marked "seen live", it may already be fixed; re-check before acting.
- Environment note for other auditors: the six tabs on `localhost:5360` each hold an SSE stream, which exhausts Chrome's 6-sockets-per-host limit, so `fetch`/page loads hang with no status. I worked around it with a raw TCP forwarder (`scratchpad/tcpproxy.js`, port 5371 -> 5360; cookies are shared across ports; stopped after the audit, `node tcpproxy.js 5371 5360` restarts it). My tab is tab-5 and is back at desktop size.

Severity: H = breaks an owner rule or the same thing is drawn 3+ ways on many screens; M = visible inconsistency a user will notice; L = polish.

## A. Same concept, different words

### [H] Menu name and page title disagree on 20 of 44 People screens
**Where:** the 20 listed in Evidence (the other 24 match).
**Evidence (sidebar label -> H1 on desk, seen on screen):** Overview & Requests -> Overview; Employee List -> Employees; Transfers & Changes -> Employee Changes; Time Off Requests -> Time Off; Time Off Balances -> Leave Balances; Claim Allowances -> Yearly Allowances; Balances (Benefit) -> Employee Benefit; Payroll Runs -> Payroll; Tax & BPJS Report -> PPh 21 & BPJS; 1721-A1 -> Annual Tax; Company & NPP -> Company; Job Levels & Grades -> Levels & Grades; Mobile & Locations -> Mobile Attendance; Shifts & Breaks -> Shifts; Time Off Types -> Leave Types; Overtime Rules -> Overtime (same H1 as the daily Overtime list); Schedule & Pro-rate -> Payroll Schedule; Components & Structures -> Salary Structures & Components; BPJS & Tax Rates -> Tax & BPJS Rates (word order flipped); Onboarding Tasks -> Checklists (and the page holds onboarding AND offboarding tasks). The department itself is "HR" in the sidebar header, "People" in the crumb on every page, and "People" in the Job Roles section chips.
**Inconsistent with:** the screens whose nav label equals the H1 (Claims, Loans...). `PageHeader.useEchoesTopBar` hides the echo on phone only when the strings match, so on the 20 mismatched pages the phone shows two titles (top bar 17px + page 15px).
**Suggested standard:** one name per screen: H1 = nav label (the nav labels are the owner-approved Talenta groups). One department name (HR in the sidebar, crumb "HR" or drop the crumb).

### [H] "Leave" vs "Time off" for the same module
**Where:** sidebar, H1s, buttons, employee sheet, Overview, Employee App settings, ESS.
**Evidence:** sidebar "Time Off Requests / Time Off Balances / Time Off Types"; H1 "Time Off", "Leave Balances", "Leave Types"; section "Kinds of leave"; button "Request leave"; Overview card "Leave" and "Leave waiting on a decision"; employee sheet tab "Leave"; Employee App module "Leave"; Team Calendar empty "Nobody is away"; ESS "Leave", "Apply" (home), "Apply for leave" (/me/leave), form H1 "Request Leave", submit "Send request".

**Where:** Overview > leave request sheet, correction sheet, swap sheet (`requests.tsx` VerdictButtons); Time Off Requests sheet (`time-off.tsx`); Employee Changes + Detail requests sheets (`changes.tsx`); Claims sheet (`claims.tsx` DecidePanel); Payroll run (`payroll.tsx`) (all from code, no pending data on the rig).
