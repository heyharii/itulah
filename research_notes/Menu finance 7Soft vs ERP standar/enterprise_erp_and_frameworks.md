## Q1. What are the top-level finance menus, modules or work areas in each system, and what sits under each?

### Takeaway
All three ERPs put a menu or work area per **subledger or function** at the top level: General Ledger / General Accounting, Receivables, Payables, Cash and Bank, Fixed Assets, Tax. Role-based **workspaces** (D365), **dashboards and work areas** (Oracle) or **spaces and pages** (SAP Fiori) sit on top of that. None of the three uses a business-cycle top level like 7Soft's Pembelian/Penjualan. The cycle grouping only appears in the process frameworks: Microsoft's Order to cash / Source to pay / Record to report, and APQC's revenue-accounting versus AP split.

## Q2. Where do AR documents (customer invoices, payments, statements, aging) and AP documents (vendor invoices, payments, ledger, aging) sit relative to Sales and Purchasing? Who owns the invoice document?

### Takeaway
In the ERP menus, the **order** documents belong to Sales or Procurement. The **open item, payment, settlement and balance** belong to AR and AP, which are finance modules. The **invoice** straddles the two:
- Sales-order invoices are produced from the sales process and post automatically into AR/GL.
- Non-order invoices (D365 free text invoice, SAP FI invoice FB70, Oracle Billing transactions) are created in the finance AR module.
- Vendor invoices sit in AP in D365 and Oracle.

The frameworks split the other way. APQC puts invoicing, AR and AP all under Manage Financial Resources. Microsoft's catalog places AR inside *Order to cash*, and vendor invoices and payments inside *Source to pay*. The latter matches 7Soft (piutang under Penjualan, hutang under Pembelian).

### Cited Findings

**Dynamics 365**
- Free text invoices (customer invoices with no sales order) are created at `Accounts receivable > Invoices > All free text invoices`, against a main account and with sales tax and charges. — [MS Learn: Create a free text invoice](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/accounts-receivable/create-free-text-invoice-new)
- Sales orders post to the GL automatically when delivered or invoiced. Purchase orders post when received or invoiced. AR is described as part of Order to cash, and vendor invoice processing and AP as part of Source to pay. — [MS Learn: Record to report overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/guidance/business-processes/record-to-report-overview)
- Vendor invoices from confirmed POs are created on the **Open vendor invoices** and **Pending vendor invoices** pages and matched to product receipts. Non-PO invoices go through the vendor invoice register, invoice approval journal or invoice journal. There is also an **Invoice capture** OCR solution and a **Vendor invoice entry** workspace. Workflows are configured under `Accounts payable > Setup > Accounts payable workflows`. The page also documents a **Vendor open transactions** report: open items per vendor as of a date, used to reconcile the vendor subledger to the ledger in audits. — [MS Learn: Vendor invoices overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/accounts-payable/vendor-invoices-overview)
- Customer payments are entered at `Accounts receivable > Payments > Customer payment journal` and vendor payments at `Accounts payable > Payments > Payment journal`. — [MS Learn training: Process a postdated check](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/configure-process-postdated-checks-dyn365-finance/4-process)
- Third-party: the **Customer payments** workspace bundles payment entry, past-due customers, the customer aging report and unposted payment journals. — [Western Computer blog](https://www.westerncomputer.com/resources/blog/time-saving-tips-part-1-pre-defined-workspaces-in-dynamics-365-for-finance-and-operations)

## Q3. Where do cash and bank documents live (receipts, payments, transfers, petty cash, bank statements, reconciliation)? Where do post-dated cheques live? (Equivalent of Indonesian bilyet giro / BG clearing)

### Takeaway
Bank accounts, bank statements and reconciliation live in the cash/bank module everywhere: D365 `Cash and bank management`, Oracle Cash Management, SAP bank accounting with the Cash Manager / Cash Management Specialist roles. **Customer receipts and vendor payments** are entered in AR and AP, not in the cash module. D365's **post-dated checks** are the closest match to BG clearing:
- enabled in Cash and bank management parameters
- entered on AR/AP payment journals with a maturity date, posted to a clearing account
- settled at maturity from separate customer and vendor PDC list pages

SAP's equivalent is **bills of exchange** (F-36, FBW6; Fiori "Create BoE Receivable - China"). Petty cash is not a named menu in any source found.

### Cited Findings

**Dynamics 365 post-dated checks (verbatim paths)**
- Setup: `Cash and bank management > Setup > Cash and bank management parameters`, **Postdated checks** tab, **Enable postdated checks**. Received checks need a clearing account, and the parameters also ask for a journal for clearing entries. A method of payment for postdated checks must also be set up. — [MS Learn: Set up postdated checks](https://learn.microsoft.com/vi-vn/dynamics365/finance/cash-bank-management/tasks/set-up-postdated-checks); [MS Learn training module](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/configure-process-postdated-checks-dyn365-finance/)
- Customer PDC: `Accounts receivable > Payments > Customer payment journal`. Fill the **Payment** tab (Method of payment), then the **Postdated checks** tab: **Maturity date**, **Issuing bank branch**, **Check number**, **Issuing bank name**. Post. The doc names **Treasurer** as the role for this procedure. — [MS Learn: Register and post a postdated check for a customer](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/cash-bank-management/tasks/register-post-postdated-check-customer)
- Accounting logic: posting a customer PDC credits the receivable but debits a clearing account instead of the bank. Posting a vendor PDC recognises the liability and credits a clearing account. At maturity, **Settle** with **Settle clearing entries** moves the amount to the bank. — [MS Learn training: Process a postdated check](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/configure-process-postdated-checks-dyn365-finance/4-process)

- Other PDC functions: transfer a customer PDC to a vendor as payment (endorsement); issue a replacement PDC that references the original; cancel a vendor PDC (returned by bank, applied to the wrong invoice, or paid in cash instead); stop payment on uncleared vendor PDCs. — same source
- Cash and bank management maintains bank-account instruments: deposit slips, checks, bills of exchange, promissory notes. ### Inferences
- 7Soft's model puts all cash-in and cash-out vouchers (BKM/BKK) plus giro clearing in one Kas-Bank menu. That is "document-type-centric": every cash movement in one place.

## Q6. What design principles do vendors and frameworks state for this structure: subledger-centric, process-centric or role-based?

### Takeaway
The vendors build **subledger-centric modules** and put **role-based or activity-based surfaces** on top: D365 workspaces, Oracle dashboards with per-role work areas, SAP business roles with spaces and pages. The **process-centric** view lives in the frameworks: APQC's cross-functional taxonomy and Microsoft's end-to-end catalog, where Record to report wraps every other process. Oracle's Receivables work areas sit between the two: each is an activity or sub-process (billing, balances, revenue, credit) inside one subledger.

### Cited Findings
- **APQC:** the PCF lets organisations see their work from a "horizontal process perspective, rather than a vertical functional viewpoint". It is industry-neutral, and not every listed process exists in every organisation. — [APQC 2012](https://www.apqc.org/sites/default/files/K04087_8.0_Manage_Financial_Resources.pdf). PCF 7.4 calls itself a taxonomy of cross-functional business processes for comparing performance within and between organisations. — [APQC PCF 7.4](https://solutions.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2024-10/K014750_APQC%20Process%20Classification%20Framework%20(PCF)%20-%20Cross%20Industry%20-%20PDF%20Version%207.4.pdf)
- **Microsoft catalog:**
  - Record to report can be thought of as coming before and after every other process (a circle around all end-to-end processes). Upstream processes post to the GL automatically (sales orders on delivery/invoice, purchase orders on receipt/invoice). — [MS Learn: Record to report overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/guidance/business-processes/record-to-report-overview)
  - Order to cash spans the Sales, Supply Chain Management, Commerce and Finance products, i.e. the process crosses product modules. — [MS Learn: Order to cash overview](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/guidance/business-processes/order-to-cash-overview)
- **Microsoft product UI:**
  - Workspaces are personalised dashboards based on job profile and activities. Modules hold all menus and pages. — [MS Learn training](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/get-introduced-finance-operations-apps/4-describe-finance-operations-apps-user-interfaces)
  - A third-party book calls workspaces activity-oriented pages, with access depending on the user's roles. — [Packt: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Enterprise Edition: Financial Management, 3rd ed.](https://m.cbook.qq.com/read/1036700110/43)
  - The Financial period close workspace is organised by company, area and person, and filters to "only assigned tasks" for some users. — [MS Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dynamics365/finance/general-ledger/financial-period-close-workspace)
- **Oracle:** all Fusion Financials applications use dashboards. On a dashboard, work areas tabulate the tasks a given role must complete, and the work areas monitor processes and report status. Examples: scanned invoices queued for the Payables Specialist; new invoices and receipts for the Billing Specialist and Receivables Specialist. — [Oracle 26C: Overview of Dashboards and Work Areas](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financials/26c/ocuar/overview-of-dashboards-and-work-areas-for-oracle-fusion.html)
- **Oracle direction:** 26B adds an AI **Payables Agent** as a separate Navigator entry next to the classic work area. — [Oracle 26B What's New](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/readiness/erp/26b/fins26b/26B-fin-wn-f43769.htm)
- **SAP:** layouts (spaces and pages) are tied to business roles. Pages group apps into ordered sections, and SAP ships role-specific example spaces and pages (since S/4HANA 2020). — [SAP Learning: Creating SAP Fiori Spaces and Pages](https://learning.sap.com/courses/exploring-the-authorization-concept-for-sap-fiori-on-sap-s-4hana/creating-sap-fiori-spaces-and-pages-1). SAP's classic GUI menu is component-centric: `Accounting → Financial accounting → Accounts receivable / Accounts payable → Document entry ...`. — [SAP Help 4.6C](https://help.sap.com/saphelp_46c/helpdata/en/96/8b3e5a43ce11d189ee0000e81ddfac/content.htm)

### Inferences
- The three design axes and where each system stands:
  - **Subledger-centric navigation:** D365 Modules, the SAP Easy Access FI tree, Oracle Navigator groups Payables / Receivables / General Accounting.
  - **Role/activity-centric entry points:** D365 workspaces; Oracle dashboards and work areas by job role; SAP business roles with spaces and pages. This is the layer all three vendors have invested in since about 2016–2020.
  - **Process-centric framing:** catalogs and frameworks (APQC, Microsoft O2C / S2P / R2R) used for implementation, benchmarking and documentation, not for the menu tree.
- 7Soft's business-cycle menu is essentially the process-centric framing used as navigation. Its strength is that one cycle is in one place for an SME clerk who does everything. Its weakness against enterprise conventions: AR and AP subledger functions (payments, settlement, aging, statements) are scattered across Pembelian and Penjualan, away from Kas-Bank, where the BKM/BKK receipt and payment vouchers sit.
- For an app that should feel standard to finance users, the enterprise pattern suggests subledger modules for navigation, plus role workspaces (cashier/treasurer, AR clerk, AP clerk, GL accountant/closer), plus cross-module close and reporting.

### Gaps
- I could not open SAP's Fiori design principles page (sap.com returned 403), so SAP's own wording of "role-based" as a design principle is not quoted here.
- I found no primary Microsoft or Oracle statement explaining *why* modules are organised by subledger. The rationale above is inferred from how their docs and catalogs are structured.
