Allotment · Adjust (prototype C)

Open an allotment, press Adjust, and type. Lower a number → that part is closed short. Raise one or fill an empty box → the extra goes out as a top-up allotment linked to this one. Nothing already packed can be taken back.

It feels like editing

One grid, the same shape it was raised in. Type over any number. The page works out which legal act each change is.

Down = close short

Only what is still to pack can go. A box never drops below what the DC already packed; grey boxes are packed in full and can only go up.

Up = a top-up

The original promise stays as it was (allotment V1 §3). More goods go out as a new allotment, linked back, capped by what is free in the warehouse.

Asked only at the end

The reason for closing short is asked in the review, once, not per box. Waiting for DC or completed: Adjust stays shut, Allot again is offered.
