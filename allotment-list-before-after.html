Before (until 28 Sep)
  • Type and Status pickers read "Choose…" (a Select bug, fixed since).
  • Six stored statuses as coloured pills — In progress and DC completed included.
  • Progress: 24 ticks coloured by stage, and a % that means "arrived".
  • "960 Piece" in every cell; returns mixed in with a tag.
  • Filter panel docked on the right; the table scrolled inside its own box, and Progress got cut at 1440px.
  • Record: a stepper with Picked and Dispatched, lines without the store, pool ids, a Pick form and an always-open Cancel.
Now
  • Figures on top, Active · Completed · Closed tabs, one toolbar line — the Purchase orders layout.
  • Three statuses as a dot and a word; Waiting for DC beside it, not in it.
  • No progress bar: Allotted, Received and To receive side by side. (Switch "Progress column" on to see the old bar in the new list.)
  • Only To store here; returns live under Return to HO / Return to supplier.
  • Record: five stage figures, lines as SKU × store, Close short in a dialog.
