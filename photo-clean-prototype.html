Photo Clean Options

Cleaning service Tap a phone's Photo to try it. Nobody is asked to pick a model, a size or a slider.

A · Ask once after the photo

The photo is taken, then one screen: the cleaned photo, big. Use Clean Photo or Use Original. Two buttons, no sliders. Crop is the quiet link under them.

B · Preview with a switch

The same screen, but the answer is a two-part switch, Original | Clean, that flips instantly. Clean is selected. One button: Use Photo.

C · Cleaned for you, undo if it is wrong

No screen in between. The photo lands in the form, tidies itself in a few seconds, and says so. One quiet Show original if it got the garment wrong.

D · A sheet shows the photo; Clean Up and Crop are options on it

After the photo, a sheet shows it as taken. Clean Up and Crop are two buttons under it, both optional. Clean Up adds an Original | Clean switch; Crop shows a square frame and one zoom slider. Use Photo keeps what is on show, Retake starts again.
