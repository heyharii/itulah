## Bugs found (tests stay red, tagged `bug`)

* **OUT-101** — the wand reopens inside the sub-menu the last fill used (quick-fill-menu.tsx: `run()` calls `close()` = setOpen(false) directly, so `onOpenChange` never resets `view`/`query`).
* **OUT-75** — counting 5 where 3 were packed returns `received 3, extra 0`: the 2 surplus pieces vanish without a trace.
* **OUT-76** — a received delivery stays on the Dispatch lane (the DC node keeps the shipment IN_TRANSIT) while the Received lane lists it.

## Blocked / not covered

* **Cloud-hosted Gudang branches** (OUT-44, OUT-90…95): amend-in-place (Edit = same grid, Was/Now/Save changes), floor of what is sent, Cancel with work owed (ticked acknowledgement, `/allotments/{id}/work`), Move back, the hard stop on a carton MD took out, Short pick strip and Close remaining. The rig runs an edge DC: `/api/v1/dc/short-picks|owed|move-back` answer 404 and `/work` answers `409 Edge-owned allotment is worked by its DC`. Written, skipped with `test.skip(RIG_DC_HOSTING !== 'cloud')`.
* **"Cannot continue picking"** is not a label anywhere in the hub: the report is the *Why?* chips on the Picklist sheet (OUT-42/43/44).
* **Edge short-pick report**: on this rig a short pick books the pieces and leaves the shortfall on the picklist (OUT-43) but the report `POST /api/v1/dc/short-picks` is a 404, so the sheet shows its Retry banner; the flag/strip/Close remaining chain cannot be driven here.
* **Overdue tab with real data** needs the business clock moved forward (Time travel: cloud controls off on the rig).
* **Still needed** with an open row needs a count that booked pieces Missing (inventory domain) or a cloud short pick.
* **Return to HO with real stock**: the rig has no store return stock (till returns/POS domain); only the screens and the refusal for a store manager are tested (OUT-80…83). Return from Store board has no live return to show.
* **Multiple suppliers in one New allotment** (Previous / Next, "n of m") and **pointer drag-and-drop** between board lanes are not scripted.
* **Phone layout** of these screens is out of scope here.
* **Stores 02…16 have no node**: only store-01 can receive the truck (OUT-70…76); the others stay IN_TRANSIT at the DC.
